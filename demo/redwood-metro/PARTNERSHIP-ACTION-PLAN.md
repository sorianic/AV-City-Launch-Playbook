---

## Objective

Convert priority stakeholder relationships into specific initiatives with:

**Purpose → Owner → Deliverable → Dependency → Measure → Deadline**

The goal is not partnership for its own sake.

Each initiative should either:

1. Reduce a launch-readiness gap,
2. Create measurable local value, or
3. Accomplish both.

# Initiative 01 — First-Responder Familiarization Program

## Problem

Redwood Metro first responders have limited experience interacting with autonomous vehicles.

An emergency should not be their first introduction to the technology.

## Proposed Partners

- Redwood Police Department
- Redwood Fire & Rescue
- Redwood County EMS
- Redwood Emergency Communications Center
- Redwood County Emergency Management

## AV Operator Contribution

- Vehicle familiarization
- Emergency-access demonstrations
- Contact and escalation pathways
- Disabled-vehicle procedures
- Scenario support
- Training materials

## Partner Contribution

- Emergency-response expertise
- Scenario development
- Dispatch workflow feedback
- Procedure validation
- Lessons from field operations

## Deliverables

- [ ] First-responder quick-reference guide
- [ ] Police familiarization session
- [ ] Fire/EMS vehicle-access demonstration
- [ ] Dispatch procedure review
- [ ] Multi-agency scenario exercise
- [ ] Post-exercise corrective-action review

## Measures

| KPI | Target |
|---|---:|
| Agencies participating | 5 |
| Personnel trained | 100+ |
| Scenario exercises | 2 |
| Critical procedure gaps unresolved at T-30 | 0 |

## Exit Condition

Public Safety readiness must meet the AVCLP launch threshold before deployment.

# Initiative 03 — AV Applied Mobility Challenge

Local universities contain research and student talent that could contribute to mobility questions while giving students exposure to an emerging industry.

## Proposed Partners

- Metro Technical University

- Accessibility

# Initiative 04 — Accessibility Experience Program

## Problem

Accessibility considerations should influence deployment planning before commercial launch rather than being evaluated only after service begins.

## Proposed Partners

- Redwood Independent Living Coalition
- Redwood Mobility Access Council
- Redwood Senior Mobility Network

## Activities

- Vehicle demonstrations
- Rider journey walkthroughs
- Pick-up/drop-off review
- Accessibility workshops
- Structured feedback sessions
- Follow-up review

## Feedback Process

```text
EXPERIENCE
    ↓
FEEDBACK
    ↓
CATEGORIZE
    ↓
ASSIGN OWNER
    ↓
REVIEW
    ↓
ACTION / RESPONSE
    ↓
VERIFY
```

## Deliverables

- [ ] Accessibility demonstration
- [ ] Rider journey review
- [ ] Structured feedback log
- [ ] Assigned action owners
- [ ] Pre-launch resolution review

## Measures

| KPI | Target |
|---|---:|
| Accessibility organizations participating | 3 |
| Structured feedback items reviewed | 100% |
| High-priority issues with assigned owners | 100% |
| Critical unresolved accessibility issues at launch | 0 |

# T-120 Partnership Review

Each initiative receives one of four statuses:

### ON TRACK

Deliverables and dependencies are progressing as planned.

### WATCH

Progress exists, but a deadline, dependency, or resource issue requires attention.

### BLOCKED

The initiative cannot progress without intervention.

### COMPLETE

Required deliverables and evidence have been completed.

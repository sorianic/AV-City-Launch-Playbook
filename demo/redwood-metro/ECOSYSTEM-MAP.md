# Redwood Metro Ecosystem & Partner Map

**Scenario:** RM-01  
**AVCLP Phase:** T-150 — MAP  
**Status:** Synthetic / Fictional  
**Target Launch:** October 2027

> This artifact converts the Redwood Metro stakeholder ecosystem into an operational register identifying who should be involved, why they matter, what each side could contribute, and which relationships are most important to launch readiness.

---

## Objective

The T-180 assessment determined that Redwood Metro presents a potential market opportunity but is not ready for commercial autonomous vehicle deployment.

The T-150 MAP phase answers the next question:

> **Who needs to be involved to close the identified readiness gaps?**

The purpose is not simply to identify stakeholders.

Each stakeholder should have a clear connection to:

- Operational readiness
- Public-safety preparedness
- Workforce development
- Accessibility
- Community engagement
- Infrastructure
- Local economic participation
- Long-term market integration

# Stakeholder Classification

AVCLP uses four stakeholder classifications.

| Classification | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Critical Partner** | Required to address a significant operational, safety, infrastructure, or launch dependency |
| **Strategic Partner** | Could significantly strengthen deployment or create long-term local value |
| **Community Partner** | Provides community perspective, representation, access, or feedback |
| **Informational Stakeholder** | Requires communication or awareness but may not require an active operational role |

Classification does not represent organizational importance.

It represents the stakeholder's proposed relationship to this specific fictional deployment.

# Relationship Ownership

Every active relationship should have an internal owner.

Suggested AVCLP roles:

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Market Integration Lead | Overall market-entry coordination |
| Operations Lead | Operational readiness and fleet requirements |
| Public Safety Lead | First-responder coordination and exercises |
| Workforce Lead | Hiring, training, and education partnerships |
| Community Lead | Community engagement and feedback |
| Accessibility Lead | Accessibility organizations and service feedback |
| Infrastructure Lead | Facilities, charging, energy, and connectivity |
| Partnership Lead | Universities, suppliers, and strategic relationships |

A stakeholder without an owner is not an active relationship.

---

# Relationship Status

AVCLP uses five simple relationship states:

```text
IDENTIFIED
    ↓
CONTACTED
    ↓
ACTIVE
    ↓
DELIVERABLE IN PROGRESS
    ↓
READY / SUSTAINED
```

A relationship may also be marked:

```text
BLOCKED
```

when an unresolved dependency prevents progress.

# T-150 Exit Criteria

Redwood Metro can move from **MAP** into **PARTNER** when:

- [ ] All critical stakeholder groups have been identified
- [ ] Every Critical Partner has an assigned internal owner
- [ ] Public-safety agencies have a proposed engagement path
- [ ] Accessibility representation has been identified
- [ ] Transportation and transit stakeholders are engaged
- [ ] Workforce partners have been identified
- [ ] Major infrastructure dependencies have owners
- [ ] Potential university relationships have defined opportunities
- [ ] Local business engagement channels have been identified
- [ ] No unknown stakeholder gap creates an immediate launch-planning risk

# T-150 Decision

### Current Assessment

**PROCEED TO PARTNER DEVELOPMENT**

Redwood Metro now has a defined ecosystem of stakeholders capable of addressing the major readiness gaps identified during DISCOVER.

However, identification alone does not improve readiness.

The next phase must convert selected relationships into specific actions, deliverables, owners, and measurable outcomes.

---

# Next Phase: T-120 — PARTNER

The T-120 phase will select several high-value relationships and create a **Partnership Action Plan**.

Initial candidates:

### Public Safety
Create a multi-agency AV First-Responder Familiarization Program.

### Workforce
Develop a proposed Autonomous Fleet Operations & Technology pathway with local community colleges.

### Universities
Create an AV Applied Mobility Challenge for students.

### Accessibility
Conduct structured accessibility demonstrations and feedback workshops.

### Local Businesses
Conduct an AV Local Supplier Information Session.

### Transportation
Develop a first/last-mile integration concept with Redwood Regional Transit.

The question now changes from:

> **Who should be involved?**

to:

> **What are we actually going to build together?**

# Redwood Metro Launch Readiness & Decision

**Scenario:** RM-01  
**AVCLP Phases:** T-60 Readiness Review → T-30 Final Verification  
**Status:** Synthetic / Fictional  
**Target Launch:** October 2027

> Readiness is not determined by whether most things are going well. It is determined by whether the remaining gaps are understood, owned, and acceptable for the proposed deployment.

---

## Objective

Evaluate whether Redwood Metro has progressed from a promising market with major readiness gaps into a market capable of supporting controlled commercial deployment.

This review uses seven AVCLP readiness domains:

1. Operational
2. Infrastructure
3. Public Safety
4. Workforce
5. Community
6. Accessibility
7. Partnerships

AVCLP uses the following proposed thresholds:

| Score | Status |
|---:|---|
| 85–100 | **GREEN** |
| 75–84 | **WATCH** |
| Below 75 | **MITIGATION REQUIRED** |

A strong overall average cannot override an unacceptable critical-domain gap.

# Why Conditional Go Matters

The difference between **GO** and **CONDITIONAL GO** is important.

AVCLP does not require every uncertainty to disappear before deployment.

It requires remaining uncertainty to be:

**Visible → Owned → Mitigated → Monitored**

The Community WATCH condition remains part of the launch decision rather than disappearing because the overall readiness score reached 90.
